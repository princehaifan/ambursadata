Every estimation method puts Exeter above the UK's measured rate

Exeter values are modelled: national usage by age (University of Exeter/YouGov, Mar 2025) re-weighted to Exeter's age profile and indexed to the UK figure, plus a method based on Exeter's share of students. The age-only model ignores Exeter's many graduates, so the real figure may be higher. National non-use is 89% among people aged 65+, so Exeter's non-users are concentrated among its ~23,000 older adults.

Method, sources and caveats
  • No representative survey of Exeter residents' AI use exists. Exeter usage figures describe University of Exeter students (Exeter Students' Guild volunteer panel, Feb 2026).
  • The Exeter-wide estimate is modelled two ways: (a) national use by age group (University of Exeter/YouGov, March 2025, n=1,061) re-weighted to Exeter's 2021 age profile and indexed to DSIT's UK figure of 59%; (b) students (15.8% of residents aged 16+) at HEPI's 95% use rate, everyone else at the implied UK non-student rate.
  • The DSIT South West regional figure (table E6) wasn't available when this was built. Adding it is the most useful next update.
  • Comparisons across surveys are directional: question wording, bases and fieldwork dates differ.
  • Full sources and question wording are in the "All indicators" source, downloadable as CSV.
